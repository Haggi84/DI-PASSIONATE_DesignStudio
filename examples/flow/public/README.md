What they show:

- **Thru 100 mm, measured against simulated:** |S21| differs by up to
  1.4 dB (stepped line: up to 9 dB at its notches) — the measurement includes the two edge connectors, the simulation
  does not. That is the case for a 2x-thru (`docs/plan/phase1_validation.md`).
- **Stepped line, circuit model:** over 0–10 GHz no passive rational model
  fits (the line is many wavelengths long) and *Circuit model* refuses; up to
  3 GHz it fits with 13 poles, and ngspice reproduces the measurement within
  0.2 dB. A package — millimetres, not 140 mm — fits over the whole band.
